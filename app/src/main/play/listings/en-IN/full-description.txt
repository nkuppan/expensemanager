Expense Manager is a free, open-source expense tracker for everyday spending in India. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription and no sign-up: every feature is free for everyone, your financial data stays on your phone, and the app works fully offline.

Log every UPI payment, cash spend and card bill in ₹ in a few taps, set monthly budgets, keep track of money lent to friends and see exactly where your money goes. Use it in English or Hindi, or any of 14 other languages.

<b>💰 Track every rupee</b>
• Log UPI, cash and card spends in a few taps: chai, auto, groceries, recharges, rent
• Share a UPI payment message or screenshot (Google Pay, PhonePe, your bank app) to the app: it reads it on your phone and fills in the amount, payee, date and category for you to confirm
• Scan a shop bill with the receipt scanner
• Record expenses, income and transfers between accounts
• Multiple accounts: cash, bank, savings, credit cards and more
• Categories, notes and receipt photos on every entry
• One-tap frequent entries and payee suggestions
• Search and filter by account, category, type or date

<b>🔁 Rent, EMIs & upcoming bills</b>
• Recurring transactions for rent, EMIs, salary and subscriptions, added on their due date
• Upcoming payments on the Home screen, with a heads-up the evening before

<b>🤝 People & debts</b>
• Record money you lent or borrowed, with due dates and partial repayments
• A notification on the due date and a friendly reminder message to send
• Debts are kept separate, so they never count as spending or income

<b>🎯 Budgets that keep you on track</b>
• Monthly budgets for all spending or for specific categories
• See what you've spent and what's left
• Alerts when you're close to or over a budget

<b>📊 See where your money goes</b>
• A Home dashboard you can rearrange: summary, accounts, budgets, debts and more
• Category charts and spending trends by day, week, month or year
• This month vs. last month, with the biggest changes highlighted

<b>🔔 Build the habit</b>
• Optional daily reminder at a time you choose
• Log your most frequent expense straight from the reminder, with Undo
• Home screen widget with one-tap add

<b>🔒 Private by design</b>
• Your financial data is stored only on your device
• No account, no sign-up, no cloud sync
• Never reads your SMS or bank account
• Fingerprint app lock

<b>💾 Backup & export</b>
• Passphrase-protected backup and restore, e.g. to Google Drive, to move to a new phone
• Automatic backups (daily, weekly or monthly) to a folder you choose
• Export to CSV or PDF

<b>✨ Free, ad-free and open source</b>
• 100% free: no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription, no locked features
• Open source under the Apache 2.0 licence, so anyone can check how it works: github.com/nkuppan/expensemanager
• ₹ with Indian number format (1,00,000) or any other currency; light and dark themes

Start tracking today and see where your money really goes, one spend at a time.
