Expense Manager is a free, ad-free expense tracker for everyday spending in India. Log every UPI payment, cash spend and card bill in ₹ in a few taps, set monthly budgets and see exactly where your money goes.

No sign-up, no ads and no SMS or bank access: your financial data stays on your phone, and the app works fully offline. Use it in English or Hindi, or any of 14 other languages.

<b>💰 Track every rupee</b>
• Log UPI, cash and card spends in a few taps: chai, auto, groceries, recharges, rent
• Record expenses, income and transfers between accounts
• Manage multiple accounts: cash, bank, savings, credit cards and more
• Add categories, notes, dates and receipt photos to every entry
• Recurring transactions for rent, EMIs, salary and subscriptions
• Search and filter transactions by account, type or date

<b>📊 See where your money goes</b>
• Dashboard with your income, expenses and balance at a glance
• Category charts showing where your money goes each month
• Spending trends by day, week, month or year
• This month vs. last month, with the biggest changes highlighted

<b>🎯 Budgets that keep you on track</b>
• Monthly budgets for all spending or for specific categories
• See how much you've spent and what's left
• Get notified when you're close to or over a budget

<b>🔔 Build the habit</b>
• Optional daily reminder at a time you choose
• Home screen widget with one-tap add

<b>🔒 Private by design</b>
• Your financial data is stored only on your device
• No account, no sign-up, no cloud sync
• The app never reads your SMS or bank account: you add each spend yourself
• App lock with fingerprint protection
• Passphrase-protected backup and restore, e.g. to Google Drive, to move to a new phone

<b>📁 Export your data</b>
• Export to CSV for spreadsheets or to PDF for your records

<b>✨ Why Expense Manager?</b>
• Free, with no ads, no subscription and no paid features
• Works offline
• ₹ and any other currency, light and dark themes
• Open source (Apache 2.0) and actively maintained

Start tracking today and see where your money really goes, one spend at a time.
